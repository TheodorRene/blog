# AI-now

I don't have any big ideas with this blogpost. The intention is mostly capturing
my thoughts and the ecosystem, and then to look back at it.

I've already seen some of this with my previous blogpost where tool has mostly
been overshadowed by features baked into many harnesses. I also have some old
tweets that capture my thoughts:

https://x.com/theodorc_/status/1830558304407302269


https://x.com/theodorc_/status/1640786246145286155


My thoughts as of 12.october 2026:

No one really knows whats going on. After Opus 5.5 and the Sol of OpenAI
oneshotting applications and building stuff has really taken a turn. You just
feel it on your body when using the models (its very vibebased, ill get back to
that). People are saying maybe Dario was right in saying that software is ready
to be automated now. And still there are people talking about how "https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jevons_paradox"
more AI, will just create more jobs and the wish for more software. 

Personally I just know things have changed. My line of work is completely
different from what it was a couple of months ago, a year ago and 5 years ago.
When I started studying computer science, I found my interest in the ecosystem,
learning new stuff and using that to solve problems. My master thesis was a
bespoke "temporal graph database" that could query a graph over time. I was
going to sell my knowledge, and would keep feeding it. I would stay up to date
on Twitter, Hackernews, Reddit and whatnot. Read changelogs, follow web forums.
The graph in my head with connections between all these knowledge would be moat.
That would be what I sell. 

That part is now free. It accessible by anyone

So I would have to pivot in a sense. I'm lucky to have gathered at least some
experience not using AI, so that I could sell some of my experience. Some of my
"taste", so to speak. I can see a solution, and feel it in my gut if there is
something off with it. I can get 5 solutions presented to me and choose the one
I deem the best.
